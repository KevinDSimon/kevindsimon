Dialed Grounds Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Dialed Grounds doesn't collect your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer can't see your information.

Your coffee data. Your beans, recipes, photos and brew history are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they also sync through your private iCloud account, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos. You can add a photo of a coffee bag. Photos are used only for this and are stored with your coffee data.

Notifications. If you allow them, the app sends reminders to rate a brew. These are scheduled on your device.

Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud on your iPhone.

Children. The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
