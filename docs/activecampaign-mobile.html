ActiveCampaign

Goal

Integrate the value of ActiveCampaign with the lives of our clients by providing key features on the go, allowing them to manage their business wherever they are.

Value

A streamlined, intuitive interface lets users review important campaign and automation performance whenever they want, and the built-in CRM lets them manage their entire sales team and pipeline from the field. The app keeps business owners connected to customers, not their computer.

My role
