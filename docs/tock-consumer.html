Tock App

Tock

Goal

Create a world-class native app that keeps guests coming back again and again, driving butts in seats for our customers.

Value

Guests are dining out more than ever, but the abundance of choice can be overwhelming. The Tock consumer app makes it easy to discover and book unforgettable experiences, while helping restaurants, wineries, and hospitality businesses drive demand, fill seats, and grow.

My role

I was part of a trio stood up specifically to rebuild the Tock consumer app as fully native, replacing a React Native web wrapper. I owned all things design, and acted as product owner and set direction.
