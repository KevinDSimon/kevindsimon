Joint Consumer Experience

Tock × Resy

Goal

Bring Tock venues into the Resy consumer experience, increasing their visibility and demand while reducing the overhead of maintaining multiple products and brands.

Value

Diners get more discovery and dining options, and more value from their Resy credit through the channels they already use. For American Express, a wider network of the best restaurants delivers membership value to existing members and entices new card signups.

My role

I was a lead designer on the search and availability squad, working on a cross-functional team with product and engineering.
