Manager App

Tock

Goal

Provide a simple, reliable tool that helps owners, operators, and teams stay prepared for every service, supporting the delivery of exceptional hospitality.

Value

Exceptional hospitality and personalized experiences don't happen by accident. They require thoughtful preparation. Tock Manager simplifies reviewing guest details and planning ahead, helping teams execute each service with confidence so staff can focus on what matters most: delivering hospitality that stands out.

My role

I was the only designer on a small innovation team with two senior iOS engineers. It was a new product, so I owned research, concepts, validation, final designs, and measurement, and acted as product owner for requirements and direction.
